About

Industry Experience. Practical Perspective.

Burke Technical Resource Group (BTRG) is an independent advisory practice providing strategic insight across the broadcast, media, and technology industries.

Founded on experience working with broadcast technology manufacturers, software providers, systems integrators, and media organizations, BTRG brings an understanding of both the technical and commercial considerations that influence technology investments.

Our focus is helping organizations navigate complex markets, evaluate technology and vendor options, understand competitive dynamics, and identify solutions aligned with their operational and business objectives.
